# Privacy Policy for VPN Holu

**Last updated: August 9, 2026**

VPN Holu ("we," "our," or "the App") is a Bring-Your-Own-VPN (BYOVPN) client for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

## 1. No Account Required

VPN Holu does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifiers to use the App.

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, log, or sell:

- Your browsing history or the destinations you connect to
- - DNS queries made through the VPN tunnel
  - - Your original IP address once connected
    - - VPN connection logs on any server we control (VPN Holu is BYOVPN — you supply your own WireGuard, OpenVPN, or IKEv2 server; we have no visibility into that server or its logs)
      - - Any traffic content passing through the tunnel
       
        - ## 3. Information Stored Locally On Your Device
       
        - The App stores the following data **only on your device**, using iOS's local storage and Keychain. This data is never transmitted to us because we operate no backend servers:
       
        - - VPN profile configurations you create (server addresses, ports, protocol settings)
          - - Credentials and keys required to connect to the VPN servers you configure (stored in the iOS Keychain)
            - - App preferences (e.g., trusted Wi-Fi networks, auto-connect settings, kill switch settings, theme)
              - - Local connection history (timestamps and data usage for your own reference), stored only on your device
               
                - You may delete this data at any time from within the App's Settings, or by deleting the App.
               
                - ## 4. Network Extension Permissions
               
                - VPN Holu uses Apple's NetworkExtension framework to establish VPN tunnels (WireGuard, OpenVPN, and IKEv2). Granting VPN configuration access is required for the App to function. This permission is used solely to establish the encrypted tunnel to the server you configure — it is not used to monitor or report your activity to us.
               
                - ## 5. Third-Party Servers
               
                - VPN Holu connects to VPN servers that you provide and control. We do not operate, manage, or have access to these servers. Any data handling that occurs on your own VPN server is governed by your own configuration and is outside the App's and this Policy's scope.
               
                - ## 6. Diagnostics Data
               
                - The App includes an optional in-app Diagnostics screen (connectivity tests, DNS leak tests, latency checks) for your own troubleshooting. Results are displayed only within the App and are not transmitted to us.
               
                - ## 7. Analytics and Advertising
               
                - VPN Holu contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind.
               
                - ## 8. Children's Privacy
               
                - VPN Holu is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
               
                - ## 9. Changes to This Policy
               
                - We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
               
                - ## 10. Contact
